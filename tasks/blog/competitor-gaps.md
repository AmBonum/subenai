**Scope:** Slovak-language scam-awareness and digital-safety content
visible in `google.sk` SERPs for the 10 pillar topics in
`tasks/PLAN-2026-05-19-blog-content-engine.md`.

unless tied to a hard signal (Majestic / Ahrefs SERP position cited
inline). Specific SERP positions in this document are inferred from

them as a snapshot, not a contract.

## 2. Gap analysis per pillar

Each subsection lists the top SK competitors currently visible for the
pillar's primary keyword cluster, what they do well, where they are
weak, and the angles SubenAI should take.

### P1 — Phishing (kompletný sprievodca)

**Top 3 SK competitors (inferred from `google.sk` top-10):**
1. `bezpecnenanete.eset.com/sk/it-bezpecnost/ako-rozpoznat-stranku-ktora-nie-je-bezpecna/` — present on multiple top-5 placements for "ako rozpoznať phishing" `[unverified]`.
2. `slsp.sk/sk/ludia/bezpecnost/co-vam-hrozi` (Slovenská sporiteľňa) — strong rank for "phishing pharming vishing" `[unverified]`.
3. `eset.com/sk/blog/domaca-it-bezpecnost/ako-rozpoznat-phishing/` (ESET corporate blog).

**Do well:** Comprehensive checklists, brand authority, clean visuals;
ESET's senior-tier landing pages have age-appropriate language.
Slovenská sporiteľňa has clean taxonomy (phishing / pharming / vishing
defined side-by-side).

**Don't do:** Static checklists, no interactivity. Slovak examples are
generic ("vaša banka vám napíše…") rather than verbatim recent SK
campaigns (Slovenská pošta SMS, MINV-SK pokuta, financnasprava.sk
"vrátenie dane"). Almost no inline "is this phishing? guess" widget.
ESET writes for ESET; banks write for their own customers — neither
has a neutral consumer-first voice.

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **Inline `BlogScenarioCard`** showing actual Slovak phishing
   screenshots (anonymised) and asking "scam alebo legit?" with reveal
   + CTA to `/test`.
2. **"Phishing v 10 sekúnd"** — micro-checklist tied to the 4 highest-
   conversion red flags (sender domain ≠ .sk, urgency phrasing,
   payment-link in SMS, attachment from unknown sender).
3. **Verbatim recent SK campaigns** — name the campaign ("MINV-SK
   35 € pokuta", "Slovenská pošta — doplatok 2 €", "Financnasprava.sk
   vrátenie dane") with screenshots, dates, and the SK-CERT advisory
   link as primary source.

### P2 — Scam SMS a telefonické podvody

**Top 3 SK competitors (inferred from `google.sk`):**
1. `uzitocna.pravda.sk/ako-vybavit/clanok/735822-smishing-co-je-to-…` — strong rank for "smishing".
2. `nbs.sk/pozor-na-podvody/` — generic but high-authority.
3. `mojandroid.sk/policia-sr-upozornuje-na-podvodne-sms-spravy/` —
   strong on specific-campaign queries.

**Do well:** Pravda's smishing guide is well-structured and SK-specific.
NBS hits the institutional-authority signal. MojAndroid syndicates
police alerts quickly.

**Don't do:** No call-script playbook ("ak vám zavolá človek tvrdiaci,
že je z banky, povedzte X a zavolajte na overovacie číslo Y"). No
audio examples of vishing / voice-cloning. No live registry of active
SK smishing campaigns updated weekly.

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **"Skript pre telefón v ohrození"** — exact Slovak phrases to use
   when an attacker is on the line; printable wallet-card download.
2. **Anonymised vishing audio embeds** (or AI-synthesised
   reproductions, clearly labelled) with red-flag highlighting.
3. **Active-campaign timeline** auto-updated from SK-CERT + Polícia SR
   alerts — "tento týždeň sa šíri X" — single source of truth for
   journalists and family WhatsApp groups.

### P3 — Fake e-shopy a marketplace podvody

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. `soi.sk/informacie-pre-verejnost/internetove-obchody/rizikove-internetove-obchody` — primary authority on the list.
2. `e.dennikn.sk/4330279/na-slovensku-su-stovky-falosnych-e-shopov-…` — strong investigative piece, paywalled.
3. `homecredit.sk/blog/nakup-vo-falosnom-e-shope-vas-vyjde-draho-…` — brand-blog checklist.

**Do well:** SOI owns the **authoritative blacklist**. Denník N has the
investigative angle. Home Credit / Raiffeisen have polished checklists.

**Don't do:** SOI's UX is institutional — the list is a static HTML
table, not searchable, not filter-able, no domain-lookup widget. No
one offers a **paste-the-URL-here check** tool. No SK content on
**Bazoš / Facebook Marketplace C2C scams** at evergreen-pillar quality
(only news columns at techbyte.sk and a few brand blogs).

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **URL checker widget** — user pastes an e-shop URL, we cross-reference
   SOI list + Whois age + has-HTTPS + Slovak-business-registry match,
   show a verdict. Even a v1 read-only check is unique on the SK web.
2. **Marketplace-specific pillars** — split "fake e-shop" (B2C) from
   "marketplace C2C scams" (Bazoš, FB Marketplace, Súrne) because the
   scam shapes differ. No one currently owns the C2C marketplace
   pillar evergreen at the same depth that's possible.
3. **Visual "anatomy of a fake e-shop"** — annotated screenshot of a
   real SOI-listed fake e-shop, with hotspots over each red flag.

### P4 — Sociálne siete a manipulácia

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. `vat.pravda.sk/komunikacia/clanok/760639-podvody-na-socialnych-sietach-…` — Pravda's evergreen column.
2. ESET Bezpečne na nete sections on social-media scams.
3. NBS investment-scam playbooks (since most invest scams now run via
   FB / Insta ads).

**Do well:** Pravda has reach. ESET has clean taxonomy of social-engineering
techniques. NBS owns "investment scam via social ads".

**Don't do:** Almost no SK content on **TikTok scams** (FYP-driven
crypto scams, fake brand-ambassador DMs, AI-influencer impersonation).
Almost no SK content on **deepfake influencer endorsements** with
named SK personalities (which already happen — see VÚB's deepfake-video
mention in the search results).

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **Platform-specific deep-dives** — separate articles per platform
   (Facebook / Instagram / TikTok / WhatsApp / Telegram), each with
   platform-native scam patterns and screenshots from the SK feed.
2. **"Deepfake influencer hall of fame"** — catalogue of impersonations
   of SK public figures used in investment scams (with each instance
   linked to its NBS or SK-CERT warning) — defamation-safe because we
   are documenting the impersonation, not endorsing it.
3. **Quiz embed**: "is this a real Adriana Kmotríková ad or a
   deepfake?" with reveal — works for any prominent SK public figure
   already impersonated in a documented scam.

### P5 — AI a moderné podvody (deepfake, voice cloning, AI phishing)

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. `vub.sk/.../vylepsene-deepfake-videa-zneuzitie-pomocou-nfc-aj-vecny-phishing-…` — VÚB's bank-customer piece.
2. `arsen.co/en/blog/slovak-deepfake` — English-language analysis of the 2023 Šimečka deepfake.
3. `pcexpres.sk/blog/online-podvody-2026-…` — SK-language 2026 outlook (low DA, but it ranks).

**Do well:** VÚB has a fresh 2026 piece linking deepfake + NFC fraud.
The Šimečka deepfake gives SK media a concrete worst-case anchor.

**Don't do:** No SK content on **family-vishing voice cloning**
(grandchild-distress scams) at evergreen pillar depth despite this being
the highest-emotional-impact scam category. No interactive demo of
"how 3 seconds of audio becomes a clone" with safety guardrails. No
SK glossary for the AI-scam taxonomy (vishing vs voice cloning vs
audio deepfake — terms are conflated in mainstream press).

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **"3-sekundová klipka"** — explainer with safe-to-use audio demo
   showing how a Facebook video clip becomes a voice clone; followed
   by the family-password protocol.
2. **Glossary pillar** — definitive SK terminology for AI scams that
   journalists and bloggers cite as the canonical reference (link-bait
   for backlinks).
3. **"Čo robiť, keď ti volá AI-vnučka"** — single-screen response
   protocol; printable PDF download for the family fridge.

### P6 — Digitálna bezpečnosť pre bežných ľudí

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. ESET Bezpečne na nete (broad portal).
2. Tatra banka #predigitalnubezpecnost.
3. SAFELab paid courses.

**Do well:** ESET has the deepest free portal in Slovak with senior /
bajtovec / IT-pro tiers. Tatra banka has polished UX. SAFELab has
real interactive quizzes (paid).

**Don't do:** ESET's content is **vendor-anchored** — every advice
loops back to ESET products. Tatra banka's content is bank-customer-
anchored. **There is no neutral, product-agnostic consumer pillar in
SK** that recommends concrete tools (Bitwarden, ProtonPass, 1Password,
hardware keys, signal-vs-WhatsApp) without a vendor agenda. SAFELab
quizzes are paywalled.

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **Neutral tool stacks** — annual "starter kit pre bežného Slováka:
   2FA, password manager, VPN, backup" with named recommendations and
   transparent affiliate disclosure (or pure non-affiliate stance).
2. **Free interactive quiz** — SubenAI's existing 15-question scam
   awareness test embedded inline at the top of the pillar.
3. **"10-minútový víkend"** — six 10-minute weekend tasks that
   measurably reduce risk (audit FB privacy, change 5 passwords, turn
   on 2FA on email, set up Google Account recovery, …). Reads like a
   to-do checklist, not a textbook.

### P7 — Psychológia internetových podvodov

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. ESET, NBS, and ad-hoc Pravda columns — fragmented `[unverified]`.

**Do well:** Mention urgency / authority / scarcity in passing.

**Don't do:** **No SK competitor owns this pillar.** Slovak content on
the *psychology* of scam victimhood (Cialdini's principles applied to
phishing, sunk-cost in romance scams, dopamine in crypto pumps) is
near-absent at evergreen-pillar depth. This is one of the strongest
white-space opportunities (also see §3).

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **Cialdini-grounded pillar** — "6 pák, ktoré na vás podvodník
   zatlačí" with named cognitive biases and worked SK examples per bias.
2. **Victim-blame counter-narrative** — explicitly destigmatising
   articles: "naletieť nie je hlúposť; tu je prečo aj inteligentní
   ľudia naletia". Establishes brand voice.
3. **Decision-flow embed** — interactive "should I act on this
   message?" decision tree that demonstrates how attackers exploit
   System-1 fast thinking.

### P8 — Bezpečnosť pre rodičov, deti a seniorov

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. ESET Bezpečne na nete (senior + bajtovec tiers).
2. SAFELab paid kids courses.
3. NBS senior-protection initiative + Nadačný fond Telekom.

**Do well:** ESET has dedicated senior content. SAFELab has age-banded
courses. NBS has the "Reč peňazí" free senior course.

**Don't do:** ESET / SAFELab assume the reader installs / pays.
**No SK pillar speaks directly to the adult-child** ("ako pomôcť
mame / oteckovi rozpoznať podvod") — yet adult children are the
audience who actually *acts* on this content. Almost no SK content
on **family password / safe word** protocols for grandchild-vishing.
Almost no content for **teens themselves** about romance scams,
sextortion, fake "scholarship" emails.

**SubenAI angles:**
1. **"Ako hovoriť s mamou o phishingu"** — script for the awkward
   family conversation; reaches the adult child, not the senior.
2. **Family safe-word kit** — printable / sendable: agree on a
   family password that nobody else knows; usable in vishing scenarios.
3. **Teen-targeted articles** in teen voice — romance scams, "free
   Riot Points" gaming scams, sextortion response protocol with the
   `IWF` and `polic.sk` reporting hotlines linked at the top.

### P9 — Bezpečné online nakupovanie

**Top 3 SK competitors:**
1. SOI (authoritative blacklist).
2. Raiffeisen / Tatra banka shopping-safety pages.
3. ESET Bezpečne na nete.

**Do well:** SOI list, bank shopping guides.

**Don't do:** No SK competitor publishes **seasonal shopping content**

3. **"Pre-buy checklist"** — 6-item check that lives in a

**SubenAI angles:**

   (see E16.20), but the article is the canonical link.

## 3. White-space opportunities

High-leverage SK topics with **no current evergreen-pillar competitor**
(verified absent or weak via WebSearch on 2026-05-19):

1. **AI voice-cloning "grandchild distress" scam in Slovak family
   context** — verbatim Slovak call scripts, family safe-word
   protocol, response-tree decision aid. Currently only news mentions,
   no evergreen pillar.
2. **Deepfake political ads in SK elections** — post-Šimečka deepfake
   the topic is journalistic, not consumer-educational. No "how to
   spot a political deepfake before you share it" SK pillar.
3. **Slovak C2C marketplace scams** — Bazoš, Facebook Marketplace SK,
   Súrne, SBazár. Tech press covers each wave; no evergreen pillar.
4. **"Family password" protocol** — adopted internationally, near-
   absent in SK consumer content. High-shareability, high-link-
   attraction potential.
5. **URL / e-shop check widget content** — content explaining how the
   check works + linking to the widget. Inverts the static SOI-list
   model.
6. **University / dorm scams** — see P10. Whole audience underserved.
7. **Slovak romance-scam playbooks** — bank materials touch it; no
   evergreen consumer-facing pillar with platform-specific (Tinder,
   Instagram DMs, dating apps) breakdowns and the typical 4–8-week
   grooming timeline.
8. **Crypto investment scams targeting SK** — NBS has authority but
   speaks in regulator-voice. No accessible "your friend on Telegram
   shilled you a 'guaranteed 20%/month' platform — here's what's
   actually happening" consumer-tone pillar.
9. **Job-scam pillar** — fake remote-work offers via WhatsApp /
   Telegram, "package-forwarding" mule recruitment scams, fake
   "internship in EU" emails. Currently fragmented news pieces only.
10. **"What to do in the first 60 minutes after you've been scammed"
    — SK-specific** — exact phone numbers (banka, polícia 158,
    SK-CERT, NBS reporting line), exact sequence, exact wording for
    bank chargeback request. Currently no single SK landing for this.
11. **Quishing (QR-code scams)** — Bratislava parking-meter / sticker
    cases are news-reported but no evergreen pillar exists.
12. **Slovak-language "verify the call" reverse-lookup pillar** —
    teaching readers to verify a caller via the bank's official number
    on the back of their card; supported by a curated list of
    verifiable SK contact channels. Currently mentioned in passing but
    no destination.

## 4. Defensive watch list

Three forward-looking concerns about competitor moves SubenAI should
monitor and respond to.

**ESET ("Bezpečne na nete") is the single most likely outlet to copy
any successful interactive format we launch.** ESET has the editorial
resourcing, brand authority, and existing audience (vendor-driven but
substantial) to reproduce an interactive scam-detection quiz, an
e-shop URL-check widget, or a "scam of the week" tracker within weeks
of seeing it work. Mitigations: (a) ship the existing 15-question quiz
deeply integrated with the blog from D1 — SubenAI's quiz is already
production-grade and unique; ESET would need to build a new product,
not just a new article; (b) publish *cluster* articles around the
quiz so the quiz becomes the centre of a topical-authority graph that
a single-article copy cannot replicate; (c) wire the URL-check widget
to SOI's list as primary source so SOI becomes a backlink partner,
not a competitor.

**Pravda's "Užitočná pravda" + Denník N have email-newsletter reach
and brand authority we cannot match in v1.** When they cover a topic,
their version typically out-ranks specialist pages within weeks
because of legacy-press DA. The defensive play is to **stop competing
on news-cycle and start owning the explainer layer**: when a new SK
scam wave breaks, Pravda will write the news; SubenAI should write
the "ako to funguje a ako sa brániť" explainer that Pravda's article
links to. Position SubenAI as the source mainstream press cites, not
the source that competes with mainstream press head-on. Concretely:
the editorial calendar should include an "explainer follow-up"
template that ships within 48 hours of any major SK scam wave covered
by Pravda / Denník N / Aktuality, with the article structure
optimized to be the most-linkable secondary source.

**Polícia SR's "Hoaxy a podvody" Facebook page is structurally
unbeatable on social distribution.** Per SITA 2026 reporting it is
"the most-followed page in Slovakia". SubenAI cannot win the
Facebook-reach battle in v1 (no newsletter, no FB ad budget). The
defensive play is to make every SubenAI article **share-card-ready**
for the Polícia SR team to embed: clear primary source citations, an
"Embed this scenario" button on the `BlogScenarioCard` component, and
clean OG-image assets that look respectable when posted to a
government Facebook page. The long-term goal is that Polícia SR's
social team eventually links *to* SubenAI when they need a deeper
explainer than they can fit in a FB post — turning a distribution
competitor into a distribution partner.
