# Backlinks Tracker — subenai.sk

> **Owner**: SubenAI editorial (the user). Living document; update on every
> outreach send + reply + link landing.
>
> **Why this exists**: E17 (PLAN-2026-05-19-E17-backlinks.md) commits us
> to ≥5 unique referring domains per pillar within 90 days, ≥1 Tier A SK
> authority within 12 months. Without a tracker, anchor-text diversity
> drifts (Google penalty signal) and follow-ups slip.
>
> **Cadence**: log within 24 h of every send. Quarterly review (E17.9)
> reads this file to identify converters, retry non-responders, expand
> the target list.
