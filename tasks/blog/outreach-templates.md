# Backlink Outreach Templates — subenai.sk

> **Audience**: SubenAI editorial team (the user) — paste-and-customize when
> sending outreach to the 42 targets in `backlinks-tracker.md`. Slovak
> language by default; English variants noted where the recipient is more
> likely to read English (international affiliates).
>
> **Rule of thumb**: 3-sentence intro, 1-sentence ask, no reciprocation
> pressure, no follow-up nag for 14 days minimum. Quality > quantity —
> a single Tier A authority cite is worth 50 directory dumps.

---

## Template 1 — Authority cite request (Tier A)

**Target profile:** SK-CERT, NBS Pozor na podvody, NBÚ, Polícia SR
(prevencia kriminality / detská kriminalita), MIRRI, MV SR Hoaxy, MPSVR
Detstvo bez násilia, SOI, Finančná správa SR, IPčko, ESET awareness.

**Subject line:**
> Doplnkový spotrebiteľský zdroj k téme [phishing / SMS podvody / AI scamy]

**Body (Slovak):**
```
Dobrý deň,

píšeme za subenai.sk — neziskový projekt na rozpoznávanie podvodov
s 80+ článkami v slovenčine. Vašu stránku o [konkrétna téma — napr.
"phishingových útokoch na seniorov"] citujeme ako primárny zdroj
hneď v 4 našich pillaroch.

Pri vašej ďalšej aktualizácii by sa zišiel odkaz na nás ako doplnkový
spotrebiteľský zdroj — máme tam interaktívne scenáre (vyber, na čo by
si v reálnom emaile klikol), ktoré u vás zatiaľ neexistujú a hodia sa
najmä pre koncových používateľov, nielen IT-security tím.

Konkrétny návrh: link na <https://subenai.sk/blog/{PILLAR_SLUG}> z vašej
stránky <{TARGET_PAGE_URL}>. Nehľadáme oplátku, len si vážime vašu prácu
a chceme, aby sa naše obsahy navzájom dopĺňali.

S pozdravom,
{NAME}
subenai.sk
```

**Variants by target:**
- **SK-CERT**: emphasize the SK-CERT/StopHoax handle-with-care guide we
  link to in 6 articles
- **NBS Pozor na podvody**: cite the specific bank-fraud case studies
  in P1 phishing pillar
- **Polícia SR**: reference Prevencia kriminality bulletins; offer to
  cross-promote when they publish quarterly seniors' brochures
- **MIRRI / digitálna gramotnosť**: pitch as adult-literacy supplement
  (not replacement) to their NPDG curriculum

**Do NOT:**
- Demand a backlink in exchange for anything
- Suggest exact anchor text (let them write it — natural anchors)
- Send more than 1 follow-up; if no reply in 30 days, archive

## Template 3 — HARO / journalist reply (cold media query)

**Target profile:** journalists posting queries via HARO (Help A Reporter
Out), SourceBottle, JustReachOut, or direct Twitter/X queries about scams,
cybersecurity, identity theft, AI fraud.

**Subject line:**
> Re: {EXACT JOURNALIST QUERY HEADLINE}

**Body (English or Slovak, match the journalist's language):**
```
Hi {REPORTER_FIRST_NAME},

Re: {QUERY TOPIC}.

{DIRECT ANSWER — 2-3 sentences, concrete, quotable.
 Include 1 specific stat or example from a recent SK case if possible.}

Background: I run subenai.sk, a Slovak-language scam-awareness
publication (80+ articles, 240+ interactive scenarios, ~XYK monthly
visitors). Happy to provide:
  - Anonymized SK Police statistics (we have a data-share with
    Polícia SR Prevencia kriminality)
  - Quotable line on tape for the article
  - Background-only context if you need to verify

Available for a 15-min call this week at {2 TIMES}.

{NAME}
{NAME}@subenai.sk
{PHONE if comfortable sharing}
```

**Do NOT:**
- Include a link in the reply itself (HARO rules — embargo on
  promotion in the response). The link/credit comes from the
  journalist's article when published
- Pitch yourself if you don't actually have the data they're asking
  for — they cross-check and your name gets blacklisted
- Reply to queries outside your domain (e.g. don't reply to a
  general fintech query just because it tangentially mentions fraud)

## Iteration notes

- **First wave (90-day window)**: prioritize Tier A (5 sends/week, target
  3 replies). Tier C/D guest-post pitches in parallel (2/week). Skip
  Tier B until 1 Tier A converts (banks want institutional credibility
  proof before linking).
- **Anchor text rule**: never request more than 2 instances of the
  same anchor phrase across all outreach. Vary "internetová bezpečnosť"
  / "rozpoznávanie podvodov" / "subenai.sk" / brand name.
- **Reply rate baseline**: 8-12% for Tier A authorities, 15-25% for
  press guest-posts, 5% for cold HARO. Below that → revisit pitch.
- **Track everything**: every send + reply + dead-link in
  `backlinks-tracker.md`. The quarterly review (E17.9) depends on it.
