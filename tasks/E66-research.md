# E66 research notes (2026-10-12)

Verified sources; anything marked *(est.)* is an estimate.

## Legal hook
- NBÚ decree 227/2025 Z.z., in force 1 Sep 2025, Annex 1: item 5 (education and re-training system), item 66 (training incl. practical simulations for statutory body, employees and third parties), item 67 (employees demonstrably acquainted with policies), item 98 (malware protection incl. user awareness). https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2025/227/ZZ_2025_227.pdf
- Parent act 69/2018 as amended by 366/2024 (from 1 Jan 2025). https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2024/366/20250101.html
- No explicit annual frequency — "annual re-training" is a sales convention.
- Regulated entities in SK: ~3 500 (https://www.risidata.com/regulations/nis2/countries/sk), 5 500+ (https://www.podnikajte.sk/zakonne-povinnosti-podnikatela/novela-zakona-o-kybernetickej-bezpecnosti-od-1-1-2025), ~7 000 (https://www.pwc.com/sk/sk/risk-assurance-slovensko/kyberneticka-bezpecnost/smernica-nis2.html). Full compliance by 31 Dec 2026.
- Consumer law: zákon 102/2014 was repealed and replaced by 108/2024 Z.z. from 1 Jul 2024 (https://www.aksamec.sk/odstupenie-od-zmluvy-spotrebitel/). Digital-content withdrawal waiver = express consent + acknowledgement + confirmation on a durable medium.

## VAT / payments
- VAT registration from 1 Jan next year above €50k turnover, immediately above €62.5k (https://dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/indirect-tax-monthly-alert-series/2024/monthly-indirect-tax-alert-august-2024/vat-registration-changes-and-special-scheme-for-small-enterprises-implemented-in-slovakia).
- Non-payer buying EU services (Google/Meta ads) or selling B2B to EU must register under §7a (https://www.podnikajte.sk/dan-z-pridanej-hodnoty/neplatitel-dph-predaj-sluzieb-eu-povinnosti).
- Stripe SK 1.5 % + €0.25 standard EEA cards (https://stripe.com/en-sk/pricing) — fixed fee negligible at €149–790 tickets; keep Stripe.
