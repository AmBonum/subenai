## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-1:** `src/routes/privacy.tsx` (or its content source) gains **section 6 — "Žiadosti o podporu"**. Slovak. Discloses:
  - What data is collected when a ticket is submitted (subject, body, email, optional name, attachments, IP country, user agent)
  - Retention: 36 months from submission, auto-archive after 90 days resolved, hard delete after 36 months
  - Lawful basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest in operating support; Art. 6(1)(b) contract for paying users
  - User rights: Art. 15 export, Art. 16 rectification, Art. 17 erasure — all via existing `/app/legal/dsr` flow OR via support ticket itself
  - Attachment scanning disclosure: files scanned via VirusTotal; clean files retained; infected files quarantined
  - Third parties: Cloudflare Turnstile (anti-spam), Resend (email delivery). No third-party AV — attachments are sanitised deterministically at the edge (image re-encode + PDF script-stripping); see runbook §3 for the threat model.
- **AC-2:** `src/routes/cookies.tsx` gains a Turnstile entry. Slovak. Discloses the `cf_chl_*` cookie set by the widget on `/kontakt` only; necessary cookie; retention 30 minutes.
- **AC-3:** `CHANGELOG.md` entry under v1.15.0 (or next minor):
  - **Pridané**: Verejný formulár /kontakt + autentifikovaný /app/help/contact pre žiadosti o podporu, vrátane prílohy (JPG, PNG, PDF, max 5 × 5 MB, antivírusové skenovanie); admin konzola /admin/tickets s vláknom, odpoveďou a notifikáciami.
  - **Pridané**: Per-kategória notifikácie pre adminov v /admin/settings/notifications.
  - **Pridané**: 36-mesačné uchovávanie žiadostí o podporu, auto-archivácia po 90 dňoch od uzavretia.
- **AC-4:** `tasks/E48-runbook.md` (English per CLAUDE.md rule for new files):
  - §1 Schema overview (started in E48.1)
  - §2 Secrets (VirusTotal, Turnstile)
  - §3 What to do when VirusTotal is unavailable
  - §4 Re-scan an attachment
  - §5 Debugging a missing email (Resend dashboard)
  - §6 When a reply email doesn't arrive
  - §7 Adjusting notification settings
  - §8 Running E48 tests locally
  - §9 Triaging a flood of spam tickets (rate-limit-bypass attempts)
  - §10 GDPR Art. 17 deletion of support data — single-ticket and full-user (interacts with E46 dossier)
  - §11 Escalating an abuse_report category ticket to legal
- **AC-5:** `src/routes/docs/admin/support.tsx` content (the docs page referenced by AdminPageExplainer + completes E47.1 dependency for the `support` slug):
  - h1 "Žiadosti o podporu — admin manuál"
  - Sections: čo to je, ako triážovať, stavový stroj, odpovedanie, prílohy a skenovanie, archivácia, GDPR mazanie, eskalácie, krížové odkazy na /docs/admin/support-escalation, /docs/admin/audit, /docs/admin/aal2
  - Slovak; ~600 words; locale-mirrored to EN + CS per E47.1 contract.
- **AC-6:** Footer link "Kontakt" verified (added in E48.3 but cross-checked here for permanence).
- **AC-7:** Sitemap update: `/kontakt` added to `public/sitemap.xml` generator output. `/kontakt/odoslane`, `/kontakt/ticket/*`, `/app/help/*` NOT in sitemap (noindex).
- **AC-8:** Robots.txt unchanged (already permits root-level routes; admin and /app paths covered by middleware/route-level robots meta).
